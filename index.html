Welcome

Ten years of translating science into practice

For over a decade, IASRM has stood at the frontier of Anti-Ageing, Regenerative, Functional Medicine and Aesthetic Gynaecology. The 11th IASRM World Congress marks that milestone — three transformative days in New Delhi where innovation seamlessly translates into clinical practice.

Expect expert-led scientific sessions, live surgical demonstrations, immersive hands-on workshops and genuine global collaboration — all dedicated to advancing longevity science and reshaping the future of healthcare.
